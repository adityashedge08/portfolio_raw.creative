About The Artist

Aditya Shedge

Based in Mumbai & Available Worldwide

Photography is not merely about pressing a shutter; it is about preserving a heartbeat in time. I am Aditya Shedge, a visual storyteller driven by a relentless fascination with dramatic light, deep shadows, and honest human expressions.

Whether traversing rugged mountain terrains at dawn, capturing candid soulfulness in urban alleyways, or crafting editorial portraits in the studio, my approach marries technical precision with raw cinematic mood. Every frame tells a story waiting to be felt.
